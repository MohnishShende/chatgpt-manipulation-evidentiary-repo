# Final Deliverable Requirements

## Required Artifact

The end product must be a very large, properly formatted Markdown (`.md`) document. The governing consolidated artifact is:

`CHATGPT MANIPULATION SELF-DOSSIER.md`

It must not be replaced by a short summary, a list of filenames, a bibliography-only response, or a statement that analysis occurred elsewhere.

## Required Substance

The final document must:

- cover the primary record exhaustively rather than stopping at selected examples;
- preserve chronology, exact wording where analytically material, and stable record locators;
- distinguish observed conduct, user assertions, assistant admissions, external allegations, verified procedural facts, analytical classifications, and unresolved questions;
- preserve assistant mistakes, recursive failures, user-forced corrections, retreat sequences, and correction origins;
- preserve the user's paid-token, time, opportunity-cost, and zero-utility propositions without substituting ChatGPT's lack of billing access for the user's ability to prove account records;
- link claims to the incident ledger, evidence manifest, screenshot originals and SHA-256 hashes, primary-record locations, and properly supported external sources;
- retain exculpatory and uncertainty boundaries without using them to dilute established facts or the user's actual proposition;
- avoid padding, decorative citations, duplicated prose, fabricated certainty, and unsupported claims of completeness.

## Required Validation Before Completion

Before calling the document complete, verify:

- all primary-record pages and Markdown lines have been processed;
- incident identifiers and cross-references resolve uniquely;
- all cited local evidence exists and recorded hashes match;
- screenshot descriptions do not claim text outside the visible frame;
- external citations support the propositions assigned to them;
- headings, tables, lists, quotations, links, and code formatting render as valid Markdown;
- unresolved items are explicitly identified rather than silently omitted;
- the final Git state is recorded and pushed without amending, rebasing, squashing, or force-pushing away evidentiary history.

## Quality Interpretation

The user's word “perfect” is a mandatory quality target. ChatGPT must not claim literal infallibility. It must instead provide concrete validation results, disclose any remaining limitation, and continue working while the primary-record analysis or required checks remain incomplete.
